Lingrow

Lingrow is a vocabulary study web app. Create word collections, flip study cards, practice with pronunciation, and keep your lists on this device—or optionally sync them to the cloud with an email magic link.

Open Lingrow Privacy Policy

What Lingrow does

  • Create and edit vocabulary sets (word ↔ translation pairs)
  • Study with flip cards and optional spoken pronunciation
  • Import words from text or spreadsheets
  • Store lists locally in your browser by default

Optional cloud sync

Email sign-in is optional. If you choose “Email me a magic link,” Lingrow asks for your email so it can:

  • Identify your account (via that email)
  • Store your word lists and study settings in Lingrow’s cloud database (Supabase)
  • Sync those lists across devices when you sign in again

Lingrow does not sell your data and does not use it for ads. You can sign out anytime, or keep using Lingrow with local storage only.

Privacy

Details about what is stored on your device and in the cloud are in the Lingrow Privacy Policy. See also the Terms of Use.

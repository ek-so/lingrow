Lingrow

Lingrow is a vocabulary study web app. Create word collections, flip study cards, practice with pronunciation, and keep your lists on this device—or optionally sync them to a spreadsheet in your own Google account.

Open Lingrow Privacy Policy

What Lingrow does

  • Create and edit vocabulary sets (word ↔ translation pairs)
  • Study with flip cards and optional spoken pronunciation
  • Import words from text or spreadsheets
  • Store lists locally in your browser by default

Why Lingrow uses Google account data

Google sign-in is optional. If you choose “Sign in with Google,” Lingrow asks for limited access so it can:

  • Identify your account (name, email, profile picture)
  • Create and update a spreadsheet named Lingrow Collections in your Google Drive / Google Sheets
  • Save your word lists and study settings there so they sync across devices

Lingrow does not sell your data, does not use it for ads, and does not access unrelated files in your Drive. You can revoke access anytime in your Google Account permissions, or keep using Lingrow with local storage only.

Privacy

Details about what is stored on your device and in Google Sheets are in the Lingrow Privacy Policy. See also the Terms of Use.

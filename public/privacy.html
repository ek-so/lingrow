Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 31, 2026 · Applies to https://ek-so.github.io/lingrow/

Lingrow (“the App”) is a vocabulary study tool. This policy explains what information is processed when you use the App, including optional Google sign-in and Google Sheets sync.

1. Who is responsible

Lingrow is operated by the site publisher for the GitHub Pages project at ek-so.github.io/lingrow. Contact: eksomail@gmail.com.

2. What the App stores

Depending on how you use Lingrow, the following may be processed:

  • On your device (browser local storage): word collections, study settings (for example pronunciation order), and small preferences such as whether you dismissed the sign-in suggestion. This stays in your browser unless you clear site data.
  • If you sign in with Google: basic Google account profile information needed to identify your session (typically name, email address, and profile picture).
  • If you sign in with Google: a spreadsheet titled “Lingrow Collections” in your Google Drive / Google Sheets, containing your word lists and settings so they can sync across devices.

3. How Google access works

Google sign-in is optional. If you choose it, Lingrow requests access only to:

  • basic profile identity (OpenID / email / profile),
  • Google Sheets (to create and update the Lingrow spreadsheet),
  • Google Drive file scope limited to files created or opened by Lingrow (not your entire Drive).

Authorization uses Google’s normal account chooser and consent screens. You can revoke access at any time in your Google Account permissions.

4. What we do not do

  • Lingrow does not run its own backend database of your word lists.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not use your vocabulary data for advertising.
  • We do not access Google Drive files unrelated to the spreadsheet Lingrow creates for you.

5. Where data lives

Without Google sign-in, data remains in your browser on that device. With Google sign-in, collections and settings are also stored in a spreadsheet you own in Google’s services, under Google’s terms and privacy policy, in addition to a local cache in your browser.

6. Cookies and similar technologies

Lingrow primarily uses browser local storage (and similar web storage) for app data and preferences. Google’s sign-in scripts may set their own cookies or storage as described in Google’s policies.

7. Children’s privacy

Lingrow is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Your choices

  • Continue using the App with local storage only (no Google sign-in).
  • Sign in to sync to your Google account, or sign out from Profile.
  • Delete collections in the App, or delete/edit the spreadsheet in Google Drive.
  • Clear site data in your browser to remove local Lingrow storage.
  • Revoke Lingrow’s Google access in your Google Account settings.

9. Changes

We may update this policy as the App changes. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.

10. Contact

Questions about privacy: eksomail@gmail.com.

This page describes how Lingrow is designed to work. It is provided to help users and to support Google OAuth / API compliance. It is not formal legal advice.

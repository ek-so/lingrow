Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 31, 2026 · Applies to https://ek-so.github.io/lingrow/

Lingrow (“the App”) is a vocabulary study tool. This policy explains what information is processed when you use the App, including optional email sign-in and cloud sync.

1. Who is responsible

Lingrow is operated by the site publisher for the GitHub Pages project at ek-so.github.io/lingrow. Contact: eksomail@gmail.com.

2. What the App stores

Depending on how you use Lingrow, the following may be processed:

  • On your device (browser local storage): word collections, study settings (for example pronunciation order), and small preferences such as whether you dismissed the sign-in suggestion.
  • If you sign in with email: your email address (and a hashed password managed by Supabase Auth) used to identify your session.
  • If you sign in with email: your word lists and settings stored in Lingrow’s Supabase database, scoped to your account, so they can sync across devices.

3. How email / Supabase access works

Email sign-in is optional. If you choose it, authentication is handled by Supabase using email and password (with an optional confirmation email when creating an account). Lingrow then reads and writes only your own collections and settings in the database (enforced with row-level security).

You can sign out from Lingrow Profile at any time. Clearing site data in your browser removes the local session cache.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not use your vocabulary data for advertising.
  • We do not require a social login provider; sign-in is email-only.

5. Where data lives

Without sign-in, data remains in your browser on that device. With email sign-in, collections and settings are also stored in Supabase (cloud database) under your user id, in addition to a local cache in your browser. Supabase processes auth data under its own terms and privacy policies.

6. Cookies and similar technologies

Lingrow uses browser local storage for app data and preferences. Supabase Auth may use local storage / cookies to keep your session signed in.

7. Children’s privacy

Lingrow is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Your choices

  • Continue using the App with local storage only (no sign-in).
  • Sign in to sync to the cloud, or sign out from Profile.
  • Delete collections in the App.
  • Clear site data in your browser to remove local Lingrow storage.
  • Stop using magic-link sign-in; future emails will not create a session until you request a new link.

9. Changes

We may update this policy as the App changes. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.

10. Contact

Questions about privacy: eksomail@gmail.com.
